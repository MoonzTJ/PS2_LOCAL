# Answers to the local session's four questions (h1z1-server)

From the cloud session, 2026-10-30. Replies to
`downloads/PS2_LOCAL_cloud_context_2026-10-30.txt` on `claude/local-v11`.

- Source: https://github.com/QuentinGruber/h1z1-server at commit `c7bf3bb`
  (2026-10-02). It is GPL-3.0, so everything below is my own description: field
  names, types and nesting only, no code.
- "2015" = `src/packets/ClientProtocol/ClientProtocol_860` (closer to PS2).
- "2016" = `ClientProtocol_1080` (better named).
- Every layout still has to be confirmed in our client's own readers.

Two cross-checks with your findings:
1. H1Z1's server replaces `<gender>` in item model names with `Male` or
   `Female` itself before sending the attachment
   (`servers/ZoneServer2016/entities/basefullcharacter.ts`, `equipItem`, about
   lines 321–420). This matches your v12 → v12.1 finding.
2. Base opcodes shift between H1Z1 versions: Loadout is `0x86xx` in 2015 and
   `0x87xx` in 2016. So the "+2" rule only holds within ClientUpdate. Every other
   packet family needs its own native check.

## 1. SendSelfToClient (opcode `03`, one length-prefixed blob)

Top-level order (2016 `base.ts` 70–1962; 2015 `base.ts` 43–~1630 has the same
order with more unknowns):

1. **Identity and model:**
   - `guid` (u64), `characterId` (u64), `transientId` (packed u32);
   - `lastLoginDate` (u64), `actorModelId` (u32);
   - head actor (string), hair model (string), hair and eye tint (u32);
   - three strings;
   - `headId` (u32), a u32, `shaderGroupId` (u32 in 2016; 2015 has
     `factionId` here), two u32;
   - `position` and `rotation` (float×4 each);
   - an identity block (character name), a u32, currency;
   - `creationDate` (u64), assorted u32/bool/u64.
2. **`profiles`** (array, 2016 lines 129–133, entry schema `profileSchema` in
   `shared.ts` 810). Entry fields:
   - `profileId` (u32), `nameId` (u32), `descriptionId` (u32), `type` (u8);
   - `iconId` and three image-set ids (u32), two u8, a u32;
   - an array of {u32, u32};
   - several u32/u8/float fields.

   Then **`currentProfile` (u32)** at line 134, then an array of {i32, i32}.
   **This profile list is the best candidate for the empty Classes tab and the
   preview class.**
3. Collections, **`inventory.items`** (lines 151–170), then **`gender` (u32)**
   at line 172.
4. Quests, achievements, acquaintances, recipes, mounts, effects, `stats`,
   titles, fire modes, ability data, character state data (lines 174–1047; not
   needed now).
5. **`equipment`** (2016 lines 1217–1244), in order:
   - `profileId` (u32);
   - a block with a u32, `tintAlias` (string), `decalAlias` (string);
   - `equipmentSlots`, an array of `equipmentSlotSchema` (`shared.ts` 2768).
     Each entry is `equipmentSlotId` (u32) plus a nested block:
     `equipmentSlotId` (u32), `guid` (u64), `tintAlias`, `decalAlias`.
6. Implant slots, skin items, then **`loadoutSlots`** (lines 1536–1541, schema
   `loadoutSlotsSchema`, `shared.ts` 2917):
   - `loadoutId` (u32);
   - `loadoutData.loadoutSlots`, an array of entries, each with:
     - `hotbarSlotId` (u32);
     - `loadoutSlotData` (`shared.ts` 2898): `loadoutId` (u32), `slotId` (u32),
       and `loadoutItemData` { `itemDefinitionId` (u32), `loadoutItemGuid`
       (u64), a u8 (255 in practice) }, then a u32 (the slot id again);
   - `currentSlotId` (u32), which picks the active weapon slot.
7. **`characterResources`** (line 1873, health and similar), `skills`,
   **`containers`** (line 1897), more arrays, then `isAdmin`,
   **`firstPersonOnly` (bool, line 1957)** and `spectatorFlags` at the end.

What they fill in for a real login (`entities/character.ts` `pGetSendSelf`,
about lines 1129–1190):
- name, gender, position;
- inventory items;
- `loadoutSlots` (from their loadout packer);
- `equipmentSlots` (from their equipment packer);
- resources and containers;
- `firstPersonOnly`.

The 2015 schema leaves `equipement_slot` (line 930) and `loadoutStuff`
(line 1188) as empty placeholders, so 2015 alone doesn't show those layouts.

**What drives the preview and Classes tab:** H1Z1 has no class UI, so this is
inference. In PS2 terms the likely drivers are `profiles` plus
`currentProfile`, `actorModelId` and `headId`, and the `equipment` block, which
carries the profile id and per-slot data. Your "DS Player.Info not found" and
the fallback NC body fit a profile list that is empty or doesn't match.

## 2. Loadout packets at login

- **2015** (`ClientProtocol_860/loadout.ts`; family `0x86`, PS2-like: tints,
  decals, vehicle loadouts):
  - `SetCurrentLoadout` `86 03` (line 18): `guid` (u64), `loadoutId` (u32).
    H1Z1-2015 sends it on ClientIsReady with the character guid and loadout 3
    (`servers/ZoneServer2015/zonepackethandlers.ts` 290–295).
  - `SetLoadouts` `86 1B` (line 82): `type` (u8), `guid` (u64), a u32 (more
    unnamed fields follow).
  - Also listed: `SelectLoadout` `86 02`, `SetCurrentSlot` `86 06`,
    `AddLoadout` `86 1C`, `UpdateCurrentLoadout` `86 1D`,
    `UpdateLoadoutSlot` `86 1E`.
- **2016** (`ClientProtocol_1080/loadout.ts`; family `0x87`):
  - `SetLoadoutSlots` `87 04` (line 37): `characterId` (u64) plus the same
    `loadoutSlotsSchema` described above.
  - `SetLoadoutSlot` `87 05`: `characterId`, one `loadoutSlotData`,
    `currentSlotId`.
  - Their `SetCurrentLoadout` call is commented out in 2016 (zone handlers
    310–313). In 2016 the loadout arrives inside SendSelfToClient and is updated
    later with `SetLoadoutSlots`.
- **Equipment packets** (2016 `equipment.ts`, family `0x95`):
  - `SetCharacterEquipment` `95 01` (line 23):
    - character data: `profileId` (u32), `characterId` (u64);
    - a u32, `tintAlias`, `decalAlias`;
    - `equipmentSlots` array;
    - `attachmentData` array, using the same attachment entry as in
      ActivateProfile;
    - a trailing bool.
  - `SetCharacterEquipmentSlot` `95 02`: one slot plus its attachment.
  - Sent whenever equipment changes (`basefullcharacter.ts`
    `updateEquipment`/`updateEquipmentSlot`, 285–310).

## 3. How an equipped primary weapon is represented

There are three linked records, all sharing **one item guid**
(`basefullcharacter.ts` `equipItem` 321–465, `generateEquipmentFromLoadout`
468–500, packers 1003–1250):

1. **Item instance:** `itemGuid` plus `itemDefinitionId`. It is sent in the
   SendSelfToClient inventory and individually with `ClientUpdate.ItemAdd`
   (2015 and 2016 opcode `11 02 00`; possibly `11 04 00` for us by the +2 rule,
   unverified). It carries weapon data (fire groups/modes).
2. **Loadout slot:** `loadoutId`, `slotId`, `itemDefinitionId`, item guid. The
   character's `currentSlotId` names the loadout slot that is the active weapon.
3. **Equipment slot plus attachment:**
   - `equipmentSlotId`, item guid, tint and decal;
   - an attachment entry whose model name comes from the item definition's model
     name with `<gender>` replaced;
   - the equipment slot id is the item definition's ACTIVE equip slot if its
     loadout slot equals `currentSlotId`, otherwise its PASSIVE equip slot
     (definition fields `ACTIVE_EQUIP_SLOT_ID`/`PASSIVE_EQUIP_SLOT_ID`,
     `src/types/zoneserver.ts` 699–700).

Links: item guid connects all three. `itemDefinitionId` connects the instance
and loadout slot to the item definition (sent in item definitions). Loadout slot
plus `currentSlotId` decides active vs passive equipment slot.

Relevance to your log: "player does not have anything equipped in the primary
weapon slot!" plus your finding that arms-watcher slot 4 (11135F0) mounts
equipped items through the equipment-slot lookup (DAB120 → 111EA60). That
suggests the client wants a populated equipment-slot record for the primary
weapon, plus probably the matching loadout slot and item instance, before the
arms finish. That is a hypothesis, not confirmed.

## 4. Public descriptions of first-person arms readiness

I found nothing about arms readiness in h1z1-server.
- Its only first-person items are the `firstPersonOnly` bool at the end of
  SendSelfToClient (2016 line 1957; 2015 `isFirstPersonOnly` line 1626) and
  first-person camera offsets in vehicle reference data
  (`ClientProtocol_1080/referenceData.ts` 63, 304–319).
- They don't handle arms or camera bones at all. Their client attaches the
  camera with only the packets above.
- **So in H1Z1 the arms become ready from normal login data alone (profile,
  equipment, loadout, items). No special server message exists for it.** That
  points away from a missing "arms ready" packet and toward the missing
  character record, matching your working theory.
- I didn't search the wider web for Forgelight arms internals. Ask if you want
  that.
