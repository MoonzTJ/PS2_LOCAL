## Results

1. **The full WeaponDefinitions body below parses through all six real
   collection readers with zero errors and zero bytes left.** It creates 7
   nodes: 1 weapon, 2 fire groups and 4 fire modes.
2. **Installing that parsed manager as the weapon catalog** (singleton
   `417C810`) and creating item 44 with the real factory gives:
   - **selected group 0, selected mode 0**;
   - **ammo (30 clip, 180 reserve)**, which the factory derives from
     clip 30 / capacity 210;
   - both groups with their definitions found;
   - all four modes linked (keys 15/115/1015/1115, ammo index 0).
   - **Without the fire-mode collection** the same factory gives selected
     −1/−1, with mode definitions not found. That is your current state.
3. **The SendSelf weapon detail (144ECA0) overrides the selected group.**
   - Sending the capture's values with **selected = 0**: the detail parses
     fully, keeps 0/0, and sets ammo plus the per-mode word 143.
   - Sending **selected = −1 (0xFF)** forces the selected group to −1 even
     with valid definitions.
   - Codex's earlier case showed that a detail with **0 groups removes the
     initialized groups**.

   Your current detail (zero ammo, zero groups, −1) therefore wipes the
   working state. That is very likely why 86 07 crashes.
