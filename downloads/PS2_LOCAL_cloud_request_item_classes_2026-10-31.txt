NEW LEAD
Our build's ReferenceData handler recognises 11 names: WeaponDefinitions,
ItemClasses, ItemCategories, ItemClassifications, ProfileDefinitions,
ProjectileDefinitions, VehicleDefinitions, Facility, Requirements,
NeedIndicatorSettings, Deployables. We only send ProfileDefinitions and
WeaponDefinitions. The client ships NO item class / category tables of its own
(its own log has 213 "invalid itemClass=0" errors at startup), so every item's
class lookup finds nothing. We think that is why model items crash.

Our "ItemClasses" packet: u32 count, then per record a u32 key and a record of
seven u32 words. We do not know what the seven words mean.

Item classes used by our starter items: 1 (knife), 2 (pistol), 10 (spawn
beacon), 11 (carbine), 14 (grenade), 104 (rocket rifle), 108, 109 (armour
look), 110 (helmet look), 191, 198 (implants), 2210, 50776, 51173.
