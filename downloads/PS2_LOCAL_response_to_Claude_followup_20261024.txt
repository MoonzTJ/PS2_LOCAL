4. ZONEDETAILS CLOCK: SOURCE ANSWER, NO RETROSPECTIVE WIRE VALUE

gateway_protocol.zone_details serializes its low32 bits as little-endian u32
after zone_id, zone_instance and display_name_id in the recovered layout.
This is the server process's local monotonic reading, not a UTC epoch value.

The supplied log records that IndarZoneDetails was sent at08:20:47.693374,
its length and sequence, but not the actual clock value. That value cannot be
recovered from UTC receive timestamps or current process uptime alone. We have
not traced whether the client's channel-2 clock is seeded from this field.

Logging the exact serialized u32, alongside low16, timestamp construction/
queue time, UTC and monotonic elapsed time, would improve a later diagnostic.
An equal low16 alone would still not prove a causal seed relationship; a native
consumer trace or controlled fixture would be needed. No clock value or packet
behavior was changed as part of this review.
