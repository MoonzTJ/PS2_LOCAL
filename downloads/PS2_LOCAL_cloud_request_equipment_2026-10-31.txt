THE PROBLEM
Equipping items inside SendSelfToClient crashes the client while it builds the
character (right after the first-person arms actor is created, before any model
file is loaded). Bisected on Windows:
- owned items only, nothing linked to slots: loads fine;
- slot links only, no owned items behind them: loads fine;
- equipment pieces + implants equipped (items with NO model): loads, but the
  arms never become "ready" and the camera detaches;
- armour look + helmet look equipped (items WITH a model): crash;
- the seven starter weapons equipped (with WeaponDefinitions sent first, as
  H1Z1 2016 does): crash at the same point;
- changing the armour model name from <gender> to Male: still crashes.
So the crash is linked to equipping an item that has a 3D model, at login.

Our lead from the client's code: the actor's model id (an actor field we call
actor+17AC) is only set through the profile activation message's model-change
path. Since our v13.1 we no longer send the activation (it crashed then), so
that field may still be 0 when the equipped models are attached.
