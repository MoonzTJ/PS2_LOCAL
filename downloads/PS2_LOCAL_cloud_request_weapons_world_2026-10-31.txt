CONTEXT
Your character-assembly addendum was right for our build too. Read from our
client's own ADRs: the VS LA Look001 armour and helmet are actorUsage 2 with
borrowSkeleton 1; the base body (model 165) is usage 1 and owns the skeleton
(ThirdPerson.mrn); Weapon_VS_Carbine007 is usage 3 on bone R_weapon
(Rifle.mrn). So the next build sets the body model first (our 0F 3B packet:
model, then attachment) before attaching anything.

We already send ReferenceData.WeaponDefinitions before SendSelfToClient, but
with bare records: each weapon is its key (the item's PARAM1) plus a record of
the client reader's constructor defaults, and five empty collections. Our
reader consumes: the weapon map, then five more collections (we believe: fire
groups, fire modes, player-state groups, projectile mapping, aim assist, as in
your H1Z1 list). Starter weapon keys: carbine item 44 -> 17, pistol 21 -> 13,
rocket rifle 802768 -> 77851, knife 19 -> 9, grenade 44705 -> 75, spawn
beacon 7 -> 6, scanner 6015553 -> 78837.
