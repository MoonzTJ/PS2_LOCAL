H1Z1 COMPARISON (github.com/QuentinGruber/h1z1-server, GPL-3.0, reference only)
- ClientUpdate opcodes line up at +2 for UpdateLocation, ActivateProfile and
  DoneSendingPreload.
- Activation attachment entry, checked in our client's reader: 4 strings then
  4 u32 words; the 3rd word is the attachment slot key. H1Z1 has only 3 words.
- 11 37 00 is NOT NetworkProximityUpdatesComplete here: it carries a GUID, a u8
  and a bool. Our client already logs "WaitForWorldReady: waiting complete".
- Still missing compared with H1Z1: ClientBeginZoning, ClientGameSettings, item
  definitions, DynamicAppearance, CharacterStateDelta, SetCurrentLoadout,
  projectile definitions.
